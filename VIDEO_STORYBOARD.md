# Video storyboard

| Time | Visual and narration |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:12 | Overview at desktop size. Animate a small title beside the brand. “EcoCycle identifies common recyclable materials and records their estimated impact.” |
| 0:12–0:43 | Mobile scan, real photo and detection box. Overlay “Photo → region → material”. Pause briefly on confidence and the correction action. |
| 0:43–1:08 | Enter weight and display the source-linked estimate. Cut to the impact counters and history. Animate the weight multiplied by the published factor. |
| 1:08–1:30 | Pan across the locator, filter plastic, and show the distance and data source. |
| 1:30–2:05 | Switch to the municipal dashboard, filter dates and open the synthetic forecast. Use a label that stays visible while highlighting MAE and RMSE. |
| 2:05–2:25 | Review a correction, show the audit record, and land on the project name and model card. |
